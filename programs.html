Exam Anxiety Management Programme

For students whose worries about exams get in the way of preparation or performance.

The programme focuses on understanding exam-related anxiety and practising ways to respond to pressure before and during exams.

1. Preparing for Exams

  • working with procrastination;
  • anxious thoughts;
  • realistic planning;
  • organising preparation.

2. During the Exam Period

  • quick stabilisation techniques;
  • stress management;
  • restoring concentration;
  • maintaining a working rhythm.

3. After Exams

  • recovery;
  • burnout prevention;
  • working with emotional reactions to results.
group training individual work cognitive behavioural therapy emotional regulation skills

Perinatal Groups and Family Support

We support individuals and families as they prepare for pregnancy, during pregnancy and as they adjust to life once their baby is born.

  • psychological preparation for parenthood;
  • anxiety and fears during pregnancy;
  • changes in identity;
  • adjusting to life once the baby is born;
  • parents’ emotional wellbeing;
  • changes in the couple’s relationship;
  • prevention and psychological support when signs of postnatal depression appear.
group programmes individual psychological support couple or family support

The programme does not promise prevention of medical complications and does not replace medical care.
