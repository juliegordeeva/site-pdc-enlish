Evidence-Based Therapy Centre

Psychology Development Center

Psychological support for children, adolescents, adults and families. Our work is informed by current research, and we tailor the format to each person’s concerns, age, needs and goals.

  • In person
  • Online
  • Children, adolescents, adults

Services

Individual Therapy for Adults

Anxiety, low mood, stress, burnout, life transitions and adjustment.

Support for Children & Adolescents

Anxiety, behaviour, emotional regulation, ADHD, ASD, learning and parent–child relationships.

Psychological Assessments

Comprehensive assessment of development, emotions, behaviour and learning; school readiness.

ADOS-2

Standardised observational assessment, used as part of a broader assessment.

Exam Anxiety Management Programme

Practical support for managing exam-related anxiety and pressure.

Perinatal Support

Support for families during pregnancy, preparation for parenthood and adjusting to life once their baby is born.

Mindful Parenting (in development)

A planned programme for parents; details will be shared once confirmed.

Career Exploration Assessment

Using the Strong Interest Inventory to explore study options and career directions.

How to Begin

  • Message us on Telegram or send an email.
  • Our care coordinator clarifies your request and suggests a format.
  • Together we agree goals, appointment rhythm and next steps.

Approaches

  • Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT)
  • Dialectical Behaviour Therapy (DBT)
  • Emotional regulation skills
  • Trauma-focused approach
  • ABA (Applied Behaviour Analysis)

Methods are selected individually. Psychological support does not replace medical care when it is needed.

Who We Support

  • Children and parents
  • Adolescents
  • Adults
  • Families
