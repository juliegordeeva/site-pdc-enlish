Psychological support for children, adolescents and families

When something is difficult at home, at school or in relationships, it is not always clear where to start. We help you understand what is happening and work out what support may be useful.

Our services include psychological assessment, individual consultations and support for parents. We explain the proposed steps and agree on goals together.

How We Work

Our work is informed by current research. We choose psychological approaches according to the person’s needs and explain why we are proposing them.

We agree on goals together and review progress, rather than assuming that the same approach will suit everyone.

Informed by Research

We draw on current research and recognised assessment protocols, and explain the reasons for the steps we propose.

Individualised Care

The format and plan of support are shaped by each person’s concerns, age and life context.

Online and In Person

We offer in-person appointments and online sessions.

We do not promise guaranteed outcomes; instead, we work with you towards shared and agreed goals.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know which service to book?

No. You can contact us with a brief description of your concerns. Our team will explain the available options and help you identify a suitable first step.

What happens at the first appointment?

The first appointment is an opportunity to discuss what brings you here, ask questions and consider your goals. Your specialist will explain the proposed next steps.

Will parents be involved in their child’s sessions?

Parent involvement depends on the child’s age, needs and the purpose of the work. We discuss this with the family at the beginning and review it as needed.

How is confidentiality explained to young people?

At the beginning of the work, we explain confidentiality, its limits and how information may be shared with parents or other professionals. Young people are encouraged to ask questions about what this means for them.

How many appointments will we need?

This depends on the nature of the concerns and the agreed goals. Your specialist will discuss an initial plan with you and review progress along the way.

How can I find out about fees and availability?

Please contact our team for current fees, appointment availability and programme dates.
